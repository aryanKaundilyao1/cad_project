# Opportunity Reason Codes

## Purpose
The Master Opportunity Score Engine synthesizes reason codes generated by the 4 pillars (Fit, Intent, Timing, Engagement) and appends its own reason codes related to Decay and Negative Multipliers. This ensures the 100-point score is fully explainable.

## Master Engine Specific Codes

| Reason Type | Category | Description |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| `decay_factor` | Negative | Explains if the score has dropped slightly or heavily due to signal age. |
| `negative_competitor` | Negative | "Recently signed with a competitor" |
| `negative_layoffs` | Negative | "Recent hiring freeze or layoffs detected" |
| `negative_financial` | Negative | "Financial distress indicators detected" |
| `negative_engagement` | Negative | "No engagement in over 180 days" |

All of these are aggregated alongside codes like `fit_industry` or `intent_surge` to provide a complete breakdown.
