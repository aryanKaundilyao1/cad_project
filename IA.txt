The Problem
Most businesses don't lack opportunities. They lack organized access to them.
Opportunities exist across government tenders, company websites, trade directories, professional networks, and dozens of other public sources. They are scattered, inconsistently formatted, and time-consuming to verify. Sales and business development teams end up doing research work instead of business development work.
This isn't a data problem. It's an organization problem.
The Solution
JAS CONNECT doesn't sell you access to secret data. The data you need already exists publicly — the difficulty has always been finding it, verifying it, and knowing which of it is actually worth pursuing.
We research, structure, and qualify business opportunities across industries, then deliver them into a workspace built around one question: is this worth your team's time?
How It Works
1. You tell us what you're looking for.
Your industry, your products, your target market. This defines what "relevant" means for your business specifically.
2. We research and structure opportunities.
Using industry-specific templates, we collect and organize company and opportunity information from multiple public sources.
3. Opportunities are qualified, not just listed.
Each opportunity is evaluated on evidence — company quality, fit, and signals of buying intent — not included simply because it exists.
4. You work from your own opportunity workspace.
A dashboard built for your business, with the opportunities relevant to your products, organized and ready to act on.
RATIONALE — This section deliberately avoids the phrase "scoring formula" and does not expose the evidence-weighting model, consistent with the platform's evidence-hierarchy architecture. It communicates rigor without disclosing methodology — the same restraint 6sense and Gartner use when describing their qualification process publicly.

FAQs (Home Page Preview)
Is this a lead database?
No. A lead database gives you access to raw records. JAS CONNECT researches, structures, and qualifies opportunities specifically for your business before they reach you.
Where does the data come from?
Public sources — company websites, government procurement records, trade directories, professional networks, and similar channels. Our value is in the research, verification, and qualification, not in owning exclusive data.
Does the source of a lead affect its ranking?
No. Two companies found through different sources are evaluated on the same evidence — company quality, fit, and intent — not on where they were found.

Part 2 — About Page
Why JAS CONNECT Exists
We spent time inside businesses that were good at what they made or sold, but slow at finding who to sell it to. Not because the opportunities didn't exist — they did, publicly, in plain sight — but because finding, verifying, and prioritizing them took more time than most teams could justify spending.
JAS CONNECT exists to close that gap.
The Problem We Observed
Every industry we looked at had the same pattern. Opportunities were out there — in tenders, directories, company records, industry networks — but scattered across sources that didn't talk to each other, in formats that didn't match, at a volume no small team could manually review.
The result wasn't a shortage of opportunity. It was a shortage of usable structure around it.
Our Philosophy
Our value is not owning data. Our value is transforming fragmented business information into organized, qualified, and actionable business opportunities.
We don't claim exclusive access to anything. The information we work with is publicly available to anyone willing to spend the time finding it. What we've built is the discipline to do that research consistently, structure it against a shared standard, and evaluate it on evidence rather than convenience.
How We Think Differently
	•	We separate research from ranking. Research is about completeness. Ranking is about evidence. Keeping them separate keeps both honest.
	•	We don't let source determine value. A company found on Google Maps and a company found through a government procurement record are judged on the same criteria — not on where they came from.
	•	We build one platform, not one platform per client. Different industries activate different modules on a shared data model, so the platform stays consistent as it scales.
	•	We don't score without evidence. If we can't support a ranking with something observable — company quality, fit, intent signals, verification — we don't manufacture confidence we don't have.
Why We Built Opportunity Intelligence
Most tools in this space are one of two things: a database that hands you raw records, or a CRM that organizes records you already have. Neither does the work in between — deciding which records are worth your time in the first place.
Opportunity Intelligence is that middle layer. It's the research, structuring, and qualification work that turns a list of companies into a ranked set of opportunities worth pursuing.
Why Public Data Is Still Valuable When Researched Properly
It's a fair question: if the data is public, why does structuring it matter?
Because public availability and usability are not the same thing. A tender notice, a company website, and a directory listing are each formatted differently, updated on different schedules, and require different verification steps. Turning that into something a sales team can trust and act on is real work — and it's the work most businesses don't have time to do themselves.
Marketplace Vision
Opportunity Intelligence helps businesses find opportunities. The Marketplace is the other half — helping verified businesses be found. Over time, this becomes a two-sided system: businesses discovering opportunities on one side, and businesses building visible, verified profiles on the other.
Growth Ecosystem Vision
Finding the right opportunity is necessary but not sufficient — a business still has to act on it well. Our growth services (content, SEO, outreach, appointment setting, automation) exist to support that execution, as complementary services alongside the core platform, not as a replacement for it.
Company Values
Evidence over assumption
We rank on what we can observe and verify, not on what's convenient to claim.
Structure over volume
A smaller set of well-qualified opportunities is worth more than a large set of unverified ones.
Honesty over exaggeration
We don't inflate numbers, fabricate testimonials, or claim exclusivity we don't have.
Consistency over customization-for-its-own-sake
One architecture, extended by modules — not a different system rebuilt for every client.
Long-Term Roadmap
	•	Foundation — upload workflow, template system, mapping engine, structured intelligence storage (current stage).
	•	Qualification — evidence-based scoring built on the structured data foundation.
	•	Marketplace — verified business profiles and buyer-side discovery.
	•	Growth Services — expansion of complementary execution services.
	•	AI-Assisted Recommendations — built on the same evidence approach established from day one.
RATIONALE — The roadmap is written in capability order, not date order, deliberately. Committing to public dates before scoring is complete risks a credibility gap if timelines slip — sequence communicates seriousness without over-promising.
