# Jumbl Workspace — GTM Activation

## Objective
Turn JAS intelligence into outbound action.

## Capabilities (V1 Draft Mode)
- **Select Audiences:** Filter by Opportunity List, ICP, Product, Geography.
- **Campaign Briefs:** Generate briefs based on the signals observed by the scoring engine (e.g., "Outreach due to recent Series B funding and office relocation").
- **Message Generation:** Suggest Buyer Persona, Job Title, Channel, and Draft Message.

## Tracking & Attribution
Record opens, clicks, responses, and meetings. This data feeds back into Phase 10 (Outcome Tracking) to calibrate the JOEP scoring engine.
